What can you do when someone is struggling?

Take the concern seriously, listen, and help them reach appropriate support. If danger is immediate, stay with them and seek emergency help.

1

Be present

Choose a calm place. Listen with empathy and let them know you care.

2

Ask directly

If you're worried, ask clearly about suicide. This does not cause someone to attempt suicide.

3

Connect to care

Encourage a doctor, mental-health professional, counsellor, social worker or trusted person.

4

Keep checking in

Support doesn't have to be one big conversation. A simple follow-up message can matter.
